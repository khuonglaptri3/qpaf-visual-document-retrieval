**Spec:** [spec.md](spec.md)

## Global constraints

- Research only; no new model training, cloud compute, corpus downloads, or sealed-label inspection.
- Cite primary sources and distinguish inference from reported experimental facts.
- Do not choose a test set or subset by observed favorable QPAF outcomes.
- All raw research notes, source cards and workflow records belong in ../evidence/.

## Review focus

1. A mixed-modality dataset may still be dominated by one strong visual retriever.
2. Query-type heterogeneity alone supports QARF, not a specific QPAF contribution.
3. Oracle gains use labels and do not prove learnability without inference-time routing cues.
4. Existing held-out sets already opened must not be treated as a clean confirmation set.
5. New benchmark names/counts/splits and reported improvements must match actual source scope.
